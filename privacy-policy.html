1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Play X Cafe (“Play X”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, book a simulator session, or visit our venue in Arumbakkam, Chennai. By using our website or booking services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number, provided when booking a session or contacting us;
  • Details of the sessions you book, such as date, time, Xperience, and simulator type;
  • Payment and order identifiers and payment status associated with your booking (for example, whether a payment succeeded, failed, or is pending);
  • Records of communications between you and Play X, such as support enquiries; and
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and general usage/analytics information, collected automatically when you use our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create, manage, and communicate with you about your bookings;
  • Verify your identity and send one-time passwords (OTP) or verification codes where these are used for sign-in;
  • Respond to customer support enquiries;
  • Process payments for your bookings;
  • Help detect and prevent fraud and misuse of our services;
  • Understand how our website and services are used, so we can improve them; and
  • Meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Payment Information

Payments for Play X bookings are processed by a third-party payment gateway/payment service provider. Sensitive payment details — including your card number, UPI PIN, net banking credentials, or other payment authentication details — are entered directly into your payment gateway/payment service provider’s own secure payment page or app, and are handled by that provider under its own privacy and security policies. Play X does not receive or store your card number, UPI PIN, bank account credentials, or other payment authentication details. What we receive and store is limited to payment/order identifiers and the status of your payment (for example, paid, pending, or failed), which we use to confirm and manage your booking.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Our payment gateway partner(s), to process your payment;
  • Infrastructure and technology providers who help us run our website, booking system, and communications (for example, cloud hosting, email, or SMS/OTP delivery); and
  • Government or regulatory authorities, where we are required to do so by law.

Any such sharing is limited to what is reasonably necessary for these operational or legal purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to help protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your booking and account information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage your bookings, respond to enquiries, meet legal and accounting obligations, and resolve any disputes. When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

8. Your Choices & Contacting Us

You may contact us at any time to ask what personal information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to request deletion of your information, subject to any legal or legitimate business reasons we may have to retain certain records (for example, records needed for payment, tax, or dispute-resolution purposes).

9. Children’s Privacy

Our website and booking services are intended for use by adults booking sessions for themselves or for accompanied minors, as described in our Terms & Conditions. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without the involvement of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
