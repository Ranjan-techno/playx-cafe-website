About Play X Cafe

An Exceptional Sim Racing Experience

Play X Cafe brings professional-grade car racing simulation to Bengaluru. Every rig is fitted with direct-drive force-feedback steering wheels, load-cell brake pedals, and curved triple-monitor (or VR) displays — the same technology used by professional sim racing teams.

Race on 100+ meticulously modeled circuits from around the world in 200+ licensed cars, spanning GT3 endurance racers, open-wheel single-seaters, rally cars, and road cars. Real-time telemetry and lap-time analysis help you shave off seconds every session.

Fully air-conditioned, beginner-friendly, and open to ages 10 and up — Play X Cafe is built for casual friend meetups, corporate team outings, birthday celebrations, and serious sim racers chasing the perfect lap.

  • Professional direct-drive force-feedback rigs
  • All gear provided at no extra cost
  • Beginner coaching & pro racing academy
  • Private bay bookings for groups & events
